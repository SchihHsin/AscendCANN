How it runs
Ploy runs this Ploybook in 4 phases.
01
Pull GSC performance
Ploy reads impressions, CTR, and average position per query directly from the connected Search Console — no CSV in the middle.
02
Find the gap
Queries the page earns impressions for, but doesn&#x27;t use in title, meta, headings, or body — surfaced as a ranked opportunity list.
03
Weave the keywords in
Ploy rewrites title, meta, headings, and copy to incorporate the gap keywords naturally — no stuffing, no awkward phrasing.
04
Ship + monitor
Edits commit cleanly, then Ploy watches the next 14–28 days of GSC for CTR and rank movement.
Concrete outputs every time Ploy runs this.
You have pages earning impressions but underperforming on CTR or rank. You want quick on-page wins without commissioning new content. Google Search Console is connected to your workspace.
A list of gap keywords per page
Queries each page already earns impressions for but never targets in its title, meta, or copy — surfaced as a ranked, ready-to-act opportunity list.
On-page edits applied
Title, meta, headings, and body rewritten to fold the gap keywords in naturally — real shipped edits, with no keyword stuffing or awkward phrasing.
A monitoring window
Ploy watches the next 14–28 days of Search Console and reports back on CTR and rank movement, so you know whether the edits actually paid off.
