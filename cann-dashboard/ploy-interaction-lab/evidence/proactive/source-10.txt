How it runs
Ploy runs this Ploybook in 5 phases.
01
Scope the run
Confirm the site, time window, ICP definition, destination, and draft policy — and check Gmail is connected before spending any contact credits.
02
Pull + rank visitors
Ploy reads identified-visitor breakdowns, scores companies by ICP fit and intent, and drops obvious non-fits like competitors and vendors.
03
Resolve one contact each
For each shortlisted company, Ploy picks one sensible contact and resolves a verified work email — never fabricated, only deliverable addresses go to drafts.
04
Draft for review
Concise, role-aware emails with one low-friction CTA, saved as Gmail drafts. Visitor-identification mechanics are never exposed in the copy.
05
Export the queue
Every company — including no-email rows — persisted to your CRM, a sheet, or a Ploy doc with fit rationale, intent signal, and draft status.
Concrete outputs every time Ploy runs this.
Visitor identification is on and you want outbound from companies already on your site. You want one sensible contact per company, not a scraped blast list. You want drafts to review and an export, not auto-sent email.
A ranked company shortlist
Identified visitors scored by ICP fit and intent, with non-fits dropped — sized to your time window rather than an arbitrary top-ten batch.
One verified contact per company
A single sensible contact resolved per company with a deliverable work email, so outreach goes to a real person, not a catch-all inbox.
Reviewable Gmail drafts
Role-aware, single-CTA emails staged in your Drafts folder for review — created, never auto-sent, with visitor mechanics kept out of the copy.
A clean export
Every shortlisted company, including no-email rows, written to your CRM, a sheet, or a Ploy doc with fit rationale, intent signal, and status.
