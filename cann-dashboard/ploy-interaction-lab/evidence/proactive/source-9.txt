AEO questions
AI citation tracking, answered.
How do you track citations in AI answer engines?
Track a defined set of buyer queries across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude, and Google AI Overviews on a recurring schedule. For each answer, Ploy records the full response, the cited page, where your brand appears, and which competitors appear alongside it. Daily history shows when a citation is gained or lost.
Can AEO tools attribute AI citations to specific pages?
Yes. Ploy connects each citation to the source page the answer engine referenced, then keeps the generated answer and inline citation markers beside it. That lets a marketing team see which page earned the citation, which query triggered it, and where the brand appeared in the answer.
How do you measure competitor citation displacement?
Measure competitor citation displacement query by query. Ploy records which competitors appear in each tracked answer, where they are cited, and whether your page is present too. Comparing that history over time shows where your content gained citation share, lost it, or still needs a stronger direct answer.
What is an answer engine position tracker?
An answer engine position tracker monitors whether and where a brand appears inside AI-generated answers. Unlike a traditional rank tracker, it records the answer text, citations, source pages, and competitor presence across engines such as ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude, and Google AI Overviews.
