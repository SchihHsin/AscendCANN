Analytics
Track traffic and connect your analytics stack.
Ploy includes built-in analytics out of the box — no extra setup required. For deeper insights, connect Google Analytics 4 and Google Search Console directly from the dashboard.
Built-in Analytics
Every published Ploy site automatically tracks:
Pageviews
 — Total and unique views per page
Traffic sources
 — Where visitors are coming from (direct, organic, referral, social)
Top pages
 — Your most-visited pages ranked by traffic
Trends
 — Daily, weekly, and custom date range comparisons
Dashboards & Charts
Create custom dashboards with charts to visualize your data:
Line charts
 — Track trends over time
Bar charts
 — Compare pages or sources
Metric cards
 — Highlight key numbers (total visitors, pageviews, unique visitors)
Tables
 — Detailed breakdowns with sorting and filtering
Pie charts
 — Traffic source distribution
Area charts
 — Cumulative trends
Ask Ploy&#x27;s Agent to create charts for you — &quot;Show me a chart of pageviews over the last 30 days&quot; or &quot;Create a dashboard with our top pages and traffic sources.&quot;
Google Analytics 4
Connect your GA4 property to query your Google Analytics data directly from Ploy.
Setup
Go to
Settings → Integrations
 in the Ploy dashboard.
Click
Connect
 next to Google Analytics.
Authorize with your Google account that has access to the GA4 property.
Select the property you want to connect.
What You Can Do
Once connected, ask Ploy&#x27;s Agent to pull GA4 data:
&quot;How many active users did we have this month?&quot;
&quot;What are our top landing pages by sessions?&quot;
&quot;Show me user acquisition by channel for the last 30 days&quot;
Google Search Console
Connect GSC to monitor your search performance — impressions, clicks, rankings, and indexing status.
Setup
Go to
Settings → Integrations
 in the Ploy dashboard.
Click
Connect
 next to Google Search Console.
Authorize with your Google account.
Select the site property you want to connect.
What You Can Do
Once connected:
&quot;What keywords are we ranking for?&quot;
&quot;Show me our top queries by clicks this month&quot;
&quot;Check if our new blog post is indexed&quot;
&quot;What&#x27;s our average position for [keyword]?&quot;
Asking Ploy&#x27;s Agent About Analytics
You don&#x27;t need to navigate dashboards manually. Ploy&#x27;s Agent can answer analytics questions conversationally:
&quot;How&#x27;s our traffic trending this week vs. last week?&quot;
&quot;Which blog posts get the most organic traffic?&quot;
&quot;Create a dashboard showing pageviews, top pages, and visitor companies&quot;
&quot;What&#x27;s our click-through rate on Google for our top 10 keywords?&quot;
