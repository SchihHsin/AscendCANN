What it is
What is an AI website builder for marketing teams?
An AI website builder for marketing teams is software that drafts and ships marketing pages — landing pages, comparison pages, programmatic SEO content — in your existing brand voice. Ploy is AI-native: it doesn’t build the page and stop. After launch it keeps working — watching your traffic, SEO rankings, AEO citations, and competitor sites — and surfaces the next move as a ready-to-run ploy. Most AI builders generate a site once from a prompt; Ploy keeps going after launch.
Brand
Brand-aware drafting
Point Ploy at your site and it slurps the page — capturing your real content, design system, and voice — then fills the gaps with web search. Every draft is built on what’s actually yours, so pages read on-brand from the first one.
Always on

Ploy runs in the background — watching traffic, SEO rankings, AEO citation trends, and competitor sites. Most mornings start with a short list of ploys already drafted and waiting: an SEO regression to patch, a competitor page to counter, a stale section to refresh. You review, then fire them off.

Frequently asked
AI website builder questions, answered.
What is an AI website builder for marketing teams?
An AI website builder for marketing teams is a platform that drafts and ships marketing pages — landing pages, comparison pages, programmatic SEO content — in your brand voice. Ploy is the AI-native version: it slurps your existing site to learn your content, design system, and voice, fills the gaps with web search, and generates pages on demand. After launch it keeps working — watching traffic, SEO, AEO citations, and competitor sites — so the site stays current without anyone filing a ticket.
How is Ploy different from a typical AI website builder?
Most AI website builders generate a one-time site from a prompt and stop. Ploy keeps working after launch — and keeps the site coherent as it grows. Each site is built on its own component library and design tokens, so new pages reuse what&#x27;s already there and brand changes refactor cleanly across everything, instead of piling up as one-offs. Add built-in analytics, company-level visitor identification, AEO-formatted schema on every page, and an agent watching your SEO, AEO, and competitor sites, and it&#x27;s the difference between a site that&#x27;s done and a site that keeps working.
Does Ploy keep optimizing the site after I publish?
Yes. Ploy runs continuously in the background. It crawls your site for SEO regressions, watches competitor sites for new pages and positioning shifts, reads AEO citation patterns, and reads the analytics and visitor data your pages bring in. Most mornings start with a short list of specific ploys — &quot;competitor X just published a comparison page; here&#x27;s a counter,&quot; &quot;page Y is losing rank for keyword Z; here&#x27;s the refresh draft&quot; — already drafted and waiting for your go.
What tools does Ploy connect to?
Ploy plugs into Notion, Google Docs, Google Sheets, Attio, and HubSpot. Pipe content in (briefs, drafts, research) and pipe data out (analytics, identified visitor lists, performance reports). Ploy can read a Notion brief, draft and ship the page, and write the resulting traffic and visitor data to a Google Sheet by morning — without a copy-paste loop.
How does Ploy match my brand&#x27;s design and aesthetic?
It starts with a slurp. Point Ploy at your site and it captures your real design — colors, type, spacing, the works — and turns it into high-quality, on-brand sections you can review, refine, and approve. You begin from an exceptionally high bar that already honors your aesthetic, not a blank template. From there, Ploy works the way seasoned web designers and developers do: it builds a real component library and reuses those well-designed parts across every page. So the site stays consistent as it grows — the same blocks, the same standards — instead of drifting into one-off pages.
Does the AI match my brand voice?
Yes. Ploy slurps your existing site to model your vocabulary, sentence rhythm, claim style, and positioning angles — then fills any gaps with web search. Every draft is built on what&#x27;s already yours, so pages read on-brand from the first one. You can tune it further with examples and forbidden phrases.
