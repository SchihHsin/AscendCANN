Quick Start
Get your first site live in under 5 minutes.
This guide walks you through creating a workspace, building your first site, and publishing it — all from inside the Ploy dashboard.
Prerequisites
A Ploy account. If you don&#x27;t have one yet,
get started at ploy.ai
.
Your company&#x27;s website URL (helpful but not required).
Step 1: Create Your Workspace
After signing in, you&#x27;ll land in your workspace. This is your home base — it holds your sites, documents, assets, brand design system, and integrations.
If you have an existing website, Ploy can extract your brand (colors, fonts, logo) automatically during setup.
Step 2: Build or Clone a Site
You have two ways to get started:
Option A: Clone an Existing Site
If you already have a website you want to bring into Ploy:
Open a new Ploy (chat) from the dashboard.
Paste your website URL and ask Ploy&#x27;s Agent to clone it.
Ploy&#x27;s Agent will import the design, content, and structure — plus extract your brand design system.
Option B: Bring an Existing Astro Repository
If you already have an Astro codebase, read
Bring your own repo
 before importing it. The guide covers the required package versions, patchable Astro config shape, Cloudflare Workers runtime, prerendering, Code Sync, and publish workflow.
Option C: Build from Scratch
If you&#x27;re starting fresh:
Open a new Ploy from the dashboard.
Describe what you want — &quot;Build me a landing page for [product]&quot; or &quot;Create a marketing site with a hero, features section, pricing, and FAQ.&quot;
Ploy&#x27;s Agent will design and build the pages, applying your brand automatically.
Typical time:
 1–3 minutes for a clone, 2–5 minutes for a new build depending on complexity.
Step 3: Preview and Iterate
Your site is instantly available in the live preview panel. From here you can:
Refine the design
 — &quot;Make the hero bolder&quot; or &quot;Use a dark background on the pricing section.&quot;
Update copy
 — &quot;Rewrite the headline to focus on speed&quot; or &quot;Make the CTA more urgent.&quot;
Add pages
 — &quot;Add an About page&quot; or &quot;Create a blog.&quot;
Adjust layout
 — &quot;Move testimonials above pricing&quot; or &quot;Add a sticky nav.&quot;
Ploy&#x27;s Agent makes changes in real time. You&#x27;ll see updates in the preview as it works.
Step 4: Publish
When you&#x27;re happy with the result:
Click
Publish
 in the dashboard.
Your site goes live on a Ploy-hosted URL immediately.
To use your own domain, head to
Site Settings
, then
Hosting
 and follow the
Custom Domain Setup
 guide.
Every publish is saved as a deploy. You can roll back to any previous version at any time from the Deploys panel.
